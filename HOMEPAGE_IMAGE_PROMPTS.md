# Homepage AI Image Generation Prompts — Poddar Jewellers

Aap in prompts ko **ChatGPT (DALL-E 3), Midjourney, Grok, ya Gemini (Imagen)** mein copy-paste karke direct high-resolution luxury imagery generate karwa sakte hain.

---

## 📸 1. Hero Background Banner (Desktop — 16:9)

**Use case:** Homepage hero banner ke piche lagane ke liye (warm, luxury, spacious for text).

```text
Luxury jewellery brand hero background image. A warm off-white (#F4F4F2) stone and linen surface lit by soft morning window sunlight, deep antique-gold (#8F621A) tones in the subtle shadows. In the right third of the frame, a softly out-of-focus arrangement of traditional Indian 22K gold bridal jewellery — out of focus enough that no single design reads as a specific product. Large clean empty space on the left for text overlay. Minimal, calm, expensive, high-end editorial jewellery catalog style. Subtle natural texture, no text, no logo, no people. Cinematic 16:9, ultra-high resolution.
```

## 🖼️ 3. Category Tiles (3:4 Ratio)

Aap categories grid ke har tile ke liye in 4 prompts ko alag-alag generate karwa sakte hain:

### A. Gold Necklaces / Rani Haar
```text
Minimal luxury category cover image for a high-end jewellery catalog. One traditional Indian 22K gold necklace with intricate filigree work resting flat on a warm off-white (#F4F4F2) stone surface, positioned slightly right of centre. Soft diffused window lighting from the upper left, gentle natural shadow. Understated, elegant, lots of negative space. Top-down flat lay photography. No text, no props, no hands, 3:4 portrait aspect ratio.
```

### B. Gold Earrings / Jhumka
```text
Minimal luxury category cover image for a jewellery catalog. A matching pair of traditional Indian 22K gold Jhumka earrings standing upright side-by-side on a warm off-white (#F4F4F2) stone background. Soft diffused morning lighting from the upper left, subtle natural bounce shadow beneath. Elegant, minimal, high-end editorial style. Top-down flat lay. No text, no props, 3:4 portrait aspect ratio.
```

### C. Bridal Collection / Wedding Sets
```text
Luxury Indian bridal jewellery category cover. A rich royal arrangement of 22K gold bridal necklace and matching earrings placed gracefully on a warm off-white (#F4F4F2) linen surface. Soft diffused sunlight with warm gold highlights. Editorial jewelry photography, high contrast, clean negative space. No text, no model, 3:4 portrait aspect ratio.
```

### D. Gold Bangles / Kangan
```text
Minimal luxury category cover image for a jewellery catalog. A pair of traditional handcrafted 22K gold bangles (Kangan) resting side-by-side on a warm off-white (#F4F4F2) stone background. Soft diffused natural light, warm true gold color tone. Understated, elegant studio photography. No text, no props, 3:4 portrait aspect ratio.
```
