# Photography Guide — Poddar Jewellers

*Photographer ke liye. Phone camera se bhi ho jayega — mehngi camera se zyada
zaroori consistency hai.*

Website "premium" lagegi ya nahi, iska sabse bada hissa photos hain. Design,
font, colour — ye sab tab kaam karte hain jab photos ek jaisi hon. Isliye is
guide ka ek hi asli niyam hai: **poore catalog me ek hi background, ek hi angle,
ek hi light.**

---

## Setup — ek baar bana lo, phir badalna mat

**Background:** Ek hi plain background har product ke liye. Off-white ya halka
warm grey sabse achha rehta hai — sona usme garam aur asli dikhta hai. Pure white
par sona feeka lagta hai, aur kaale par silver gayab ho jaata hai.

**Light:** Khidki ki roshni sabse achhi hai, par **seedhi dhoop nahi**. Dhoop se
tez chamak (hotspot) aur gehri parchhai banti hai. Baadal wale din ki roshni, ya
khidki par ek patla safed parda — yahi ideal hai.

Dusri taraf ek safed kagaz ya thermocol ka tukda rakho. Ye roshni ko wapas
uchaal kar parchhai ko halka karta hai. Ye ek chhota sa kaam photos ko kaafi
professional bana deta hai.

**Camera ki jagah:** Phone ko ek hi unchai aur ek hi angle par fix karo. Tripod
ho to behtar, warna kitabon ka dher bhi chalega. **Har product ke liye wahi
position.** Agar ek chain upar se aur doosri tirchi dikhegi, to grid bikhra hua
lagega.

---

## Har product ke 4 photos

1. **Main shot** — poora product, seedha, beech me, saaf background. Yahi listing
   me dikhega, isliye yahi sabse zaroori hai.
2. **Detail** — paas se, nakkashi ya design dikhate hue
3. **Pehne hue** — haath, gala ya kaan par. Isse customer ko size ka andaza
   lagta hai, aur yahi shot sabse zyada bikwata hai.
4. **Scale** — kisi jaani-pehchani cheez ke saath (sikka, ungli), taaki asli
   size samajh aaye

Kam se kam #1 aur #3 zaroor chahiye.

Upar ke 4 shots **nayi photography** ke liye hain. Jo supplier ki tray photos
pehle se hain, unse kaam chalane ka tareeka `docs/AI-IMAGERY.md` me hai — wahan
3 photos hain (main, zoom, pehne hue), kyunki scale shot AI se bharosemand nahi
banta. Ek baat wahan se yahan bhi yaad rakhiye: **AI se banai gayi har photo ko
asli ke bagal me rakh kar ginti karni hoti hai** — layer, latkan, motif. Design
badla to customer dukaan aakar nirash hoga.

---

## Karna hai

- Product ko har shot se pehle microfiber cloth se saaf karo — ungli ke nishan
  photo me bade dikhte hain
- Har product ko frame me lagbhag barabar jagah do
- **Sabse zyada resolution** par shoot karo. Chhoti photo ko bada nahi kar sakte,
  badi ko chhota kar sakte hain.
- Ek hi baithak me zyada se zyada products nipta lo — light badalne se pehle

## Nahi karna hai

- ❌ Phone ka flash — sone par bhadda safed dhabba banta hai
- ❌ Alag-alag din alag-alag background
- ❌ Filter ya heavy editing — sone ka rang asli hona chahiye, warna customer
  dukaan aakar nirash hoga
- ❌ Digital zoom — camera ko paas le jao, zoom mat karo
- ❌ Bhara hua background — props, kapde, phool. Sirf product.

---

## Bhejte waqt

- **Original files** bhejo — WhatsApp se mat bhejo, wo compress kar deta hai.
  Google Drive ya pen drive theek hai.
- File ka naam product ke naam par rakho: `payal-traditional-01.jpg`,
  `payal-traditional-02.jpg`
- Resize ya crop karne ki zarurat nahi — website khud kar legi

---

## Ek baat yaad rakhiye

Customer ye photo dekh kar tay karega ki dukaan tak aana hai ya nahi. Ek achhi
photo ek achhe product ko bechti hai; ek kharab photo use chhupa deti hai.
Jaldbaazi me 50 photos se behtar hai, aaram se 20.
