## 💎 Section 3: Product Detail Page (3 Gallery Shots Per Product)

Har product ke 3 photos chahiye hote hain website par:

### Shot 1: Main Product Flatlay (Square 1:1)
```text
Ecommerce catalog photography of a single 22K gold [Jhumka / Necklace / Ring / Bangle], centred on a seamless warm off-white (#F4F4F2) surface. Soft diffused light from upper left, gentle shadow beneath. True warm gold color tone, sharp focus throughout, no background clutter, no text, 1:1 square.
```

### Shot 2: Macro Close-Up Zoom (Square 1:1)
```text
Extreme macro close-up shot of the central carving and filigree detail of the gold [Jhumka / Necklace / Ring / Bangle], filling 85% of the frame. Soft warm off-white (#F4F4F2) background falling out of focus. Shallow depth of field, revealing real gold metal texture. No text, 1:1 square.
```

### Shot 3: Model Worn / On-Body Shot (Portrait 4:5)
```text
Editorial jewellery photography of a South Asian woman wearing the gold [Jhumka / Necklace / Ring / Bangle]. Close crop on ear/neck/wrist, warm brown natural skin texture, soft natural window light. She wears a deep red silk saree. Softly blurred warm off-white background. No face visible, 4:5 portrait.
```
